Is the fundability program a guarantee of funding?

No. It's advisory work: entity cleanup, business credit building, a lender-ready financial packet, a fundability score, and warm introductions to lenders you realistically qualify for. Whether you get approved, and for how much, is between you and the lender.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do, directly to Meta and Google on your own card. My fee covers building, running, and optimizing the campaigns — not the media budget. Plan on $1,000–1,500/mo minimum spend on Growth.

Why the minimum terms?

Paid ads take 60–90 days to optimize. Clients who go month-to-month tend to pull the plug at week three and never see the result. The 3- and 6-month minimums exist so the work has time to pay off.

How fast is the website?

Live within 14 days of a completed intake, with one revision round included on Foundation.

What happens right after I check out?

You get an intake form and a scheduling link. We do a kickoff call, lock scope and dates, and the build starts. Payment is handled by Whop.

Can I start on Foundation and move up later?

Yes. Most businesses start with Foundation or Growth and move into Scale & Fund once the lead flow is working and capital is the next constraint.
