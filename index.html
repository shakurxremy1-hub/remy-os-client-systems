What do I get with Foundation?

A 5-page conversion website with copywriting and a mobile build, on-page SEO basics, a lead form that goes to your inbox or CRM, and a Google Business Profile tune-up. It includes one revision round and is delivered in 14 days. One payment, no retainer.

What happens after I book a call?

On the call we lock the offer, the customer and the scope. Then you get a blueprint covering the offer, the customer, the tier and the dates. Then I build.

Do the monthly plans have a minimum?

Growth has a 3-month minimum and Scale & Fund has a 6-month minimum. On Growth, the monthly retainer begins after onboarding.

What is the difference between OPM Ventures and Remy OS?

OPM Ventures LLC is the company: practical technology, business systems, automation and consulting. Remy OS is the product: a technology and business automation platform.

Who will I be working with?

One person, accountable for the entire chain. I build the website, run the paid traffic and get your business lender-ready. That is Shakur, founder of OPM Ventures LLC.
