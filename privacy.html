Privacy

Updated November 9, 2026

What this site collects

Nothing. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and has no sign-up or contact form that stores anything. Fonts and images load from this site, not from Google or any other third party.

The message builder

It runs only in your browser. It writes a draft email and opens your own mail app. Nothing you type is sent to or saved by this site. If you then send the email, it reaches us the way any email does.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles that.

Links to other services

Booking a call goes through Calendly. Buying a plan goes through WHOP. When you click those links, you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply to what you enter there.

Email we receive

If you email us, we use what you send to reply and to do the work you ask about. We don't sell or share it.
